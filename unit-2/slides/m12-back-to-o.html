Notes. The three loops perform about 2n^3 scalar operations, but that is not a lower bound on the problem. Strassen's recursion uses seven half-size products, giving exponent log2(7). Dupont and colleagues reported the bound below 2.371177 in August 2026. OpenAI's October preprint reports omega at most 9/4 over the complex numbers. Notes. The epsilon can be any fixed positive number; the algorithm's constant may depend on it. The stated result is O_epsilon(n^(9/4+epsilon)), not an exact O(n^(9/4)) runtime claim.
