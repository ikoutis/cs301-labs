Which words are close to a word?

def nearest(word, U, k=8):
    """The k words whose vectors point most nearly in the direction of the vector of word."""
    similarity = U @ U[row(word)]                  # one dot product for every word
    order = np.argsort(-similarity)                # row numbers, from the most similar word on
    return [(words[i], round(float(similarity[i]), 2)) for i in order[1:k + 1]]    # order[0] is the word itself

The words closest to 'piano', with their cosine similarity:
  violin          0.93
  cello           0.92
  flute           0.85
  concerto        0.84
  • Two vectors are compared by the angle between them: the cosine similarity is their dot product after each is divided by its length. The dot product is the sum of the products of matching entries.
  • U @ v computes the dot product of every row of U with v: 50,000 dot products in one operation.

Arithmetic on words

def analogy(a, b, c, U, k=3, leave_out=True):
    """a is to b as c is to which word? The k words closest to: vector of b - vector of a + vector of c."""
    target = U[row(b)] - U[row(a)] + U[row(c)]
    similarity = U @ (target / np.linalg.norm(target))
    if leave_out:
        similarity[[row(a), row(b), row(c)]] = -2      # the three words of the question cannot be the answer
    return [words[i] for i in np.argsort(-similarity)[:k]]

A text is a sequence of vectors

'the dog chased the cat' and 'the cat chased the dog':
   rows 0 and 3 of the first matrix, both the word 'the', are equal: True
   the two matrices are equal: False
   the averages of their rows are equal: True
  • A text of L words is a matrix with L rows, in the order of the words. The same words in another order give another matrix, with the same average row: an operation that ignores the order cannot tell the two sentences apart.
